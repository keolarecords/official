Keola Records

Keola Records is an independent electronic dance music label based in the US, founded by Ana Keola, also known as Sun Berri. Keola Records was established in 2023, initially as a private label. Two years later in September of 2025, Keola Records opened the label to give select music creators an opportunity to sample tracks from Sun Berri's catalog with provided production and distribution support. This decision was made by Sun Berri to help aspiring creators build their portfolio while gaining experience with networking and collaboration. Keola Records strives to create a new wave of electronic dance music and elevate the genre across Southeast Asia. Investors, creators, and all innovators who are interested to learn more about Keola Records are welcome to connect.
